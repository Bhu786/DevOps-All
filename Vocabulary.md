### 🎯 Pronunciation rule

For practice, **don't try to pronounce the whole word at once**. Break it into chunks.

For example:

**Reconciliation** → `REK | un | sil | ee | AY | shun`

**Idempotency** → `eye | dem | POH | ten | see`

**Interoperability** → `IN | ter | OP | uh | ruh | BIL | uh | tee`

**Heterogeneous** → `HET | er | oh | JEE | nee | us`

**Deoptimization** → `DEE | op | tuh | muh | ZAY | shun`

## Practice Plan (Suggestion)

- **Din 1-5:** Categories 1-5 (Emotions + Personality) — inhe roz apni feelings describe karte waqt use karo.
- **Din 6-10:** Categories 6-10 (Business, Debate, Descriptive, Idiomatic, Formal) — office/interview context mein practice karo.

**ByzantineFaultTolerance** → `BIZ | un | teen | FAWLT | TOL | er | unss`
